How reminders work

Each student gets one email covering all their units: a course notice when a class is logged, then a reminder a week before, another 3 days before, an overdue warning, and a second warning 7 days after the due date. Marking a unit Submitted stops them. Results are different: when you mark a unit Competent or Not yet competent, the student is emailed straight away (you can untick that in the dialog). A Not yet competent email includes your feedback and restarts the reminders for the new due date. Any result that did not go out, because the box was unticked, the student has no email address, or sending failed, waits here to be sent. Every send is logged.
